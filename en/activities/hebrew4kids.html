About the class

At Ogen Center, we invite children aged 6–10 to join fun and engaging Hebrew classes that combine language learning with an introduction to Israeli culture and life in Israel.

The classes are led by Dorit and Irit in a warm, encouraging, and playful environment, with activities adapted to the children's age and Hebrew level.

During the classes, children develop their:

  • 📖 Reading skills – learning letters, words, and texts while gradually building confidence in independent reading.
  • ✏️ Writing skills – practicing writing, vocabulary, and written expression.
  • 💬 Speaking and conversation – developing confidence in using Hebrew and communicating with others.
  • 🇮🇱 Israeli culture and life – discovering Israel through stories, songs, games, and events from everyday Israeli life.
  • 🕎 Jewish holidays and special occasions – exploring the holidays, traditions, stories, and customs in a fun and engaging way.

The goal is not only to learn Hebrew, but to build a natural connection with the language, Israeli culture, and Israel.

The classes are designed for children aged 6–10, with an emphasis on participation, creativity, games, and learning together in a friendly group.
