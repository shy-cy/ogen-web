About the class

Introduction to Judaism<h3>Explore Jewish Tradition, Discover Its Richness and Find Your Own Connection</h3><p>What is Judaism? Who are the Jewish people, and what connects Jews across generations, cultures and communities? How do ideas, values and traditions that have evolved over thousands of years continue to shape our lives today?</p><p>The Introduction to Judaism course offers an engaging journey through the many dimensions of Jewish life, from faith and spirituality to sacred texts, culture, history and community. We will explore the traditions and values that have shaped Jewish identity, discover how they are expressed in everyday life, and consider how they continue to evolve in the modern world.</p><p>The course explores five key themes:</p><ul><li>The Jewish People, Beliefs and Sacred Texts: Jewish identity, different understandings of God, the Torah and other Jewish sources, prayer and the synagogue.</li><li>Living a Jewish Life: Jewish values and ethics, the Jewish home, relationships and family, life-cycle rituals, education and the different stages of life.</li><li>Living in Jewish Time: The Hebrew calendar, Shabbat, the High Holidays, the major Jewish festivals and other holidays and observances.</li><li>The Jewish Story: The history of the Jewish people, the Holocaust, Zionism and Israel, and the diversity of Jewish life and communities around the world.</li><li>Identity and Belonging: The connection between personal and Jewish identity, the role of family in Jewish life, and the many ways people find a sense of belonging in a Jewish community.</li></ul><p>Learning combines the exploration of Jewish texts and ideas with open discussion, questions and personal reflection. We will encounter different perspectives, explore questions that have shaped Jewish thought and consider how Jewish tradition relates to our own identities, values and choices.</p><p>Whether you are discovering Judaism for the first time or looking to deepen your understanding, this course offers an opportunity to learn, reflect and ask questions in a welcoming environment. No previous knowledge is needed. All questions and perspectives are welcome, and everyone's contribution is part of the shared learning experience.</p><p>Join us to explore the richness of Judaism, discover the traditions that have shaped Jewish life and find your own connection to this living heritage.</p>
