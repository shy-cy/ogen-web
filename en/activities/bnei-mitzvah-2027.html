About the class

A journey of growing up, discovering who you are and where you come from

Becoming Bar or Bat Mitzvah is a special moment in a young person’s life. It is not only about preparing for a ceremony - it is also a chance to stop, ask questions and think about who we are, where we come from and who we want to become.

The OGEN Bar & Bat Mitzvah Program brings young people together for a journey of discovery, friendship and learning. Together, we explore family stories, identity, Jewish culture, growing up, responsibility, friendship and our different circles of belonging.

There is plenty of room for questions, conversation, creativity and fun. Along the way, participants get to know themselves, their families and one another a little better.

The program brings together tradition and new ideas, personal stories and family history, and Jewish and universal values.

What will we do together?

Who am I?

We start with ourselves.

What does becoming Bar or Bat Mitzvah mean to me? What does mitzvah actually mean? What are my strengths, talents and things I care about?

We get to know one another, explore our names and the stories behind them, and think about how we can bring our strengths into the different parts of our lives.

Where do I come from?

Every family has a story.

We will explore our own family stories through conversations, interviews, photographs and other creative ways of collecting memories. We’ll discover the people, places and experiences that have shaped our families - and, in some cases, bring family members in Israel into the journey too.

Our family stories become part of a bigger story

Once we start sharing our family stories, we often discover how much they have in common.

We’ll look at the connections between our personal stories and the bigger stories of our people, our communities and our heritage.

What does it mean to grow up?

Growing up is about more than getting older.

What makes us feel grown up? What does independence mean? And when do we become responsible for the choices we make?

We’ll talk about responsibility in our own lives - towards ourselves, our families, our friends and our community and think about ways to turn our ideas and values into action.

Friends, role models and the people around us

Friends become an important part of our lives as we grow up.

We’ll talk about friendship, relationships and the people who influence us. We’ll also meet inspiring people from the local community and hear about their experiences.

Where do I belong?

Each of us belongs to many different circles - our family, our friends, our community, Israel and the wider Jewish world.

We’ll explore what these different connections mean to us and how they come together to form our own, sometimes complex, identity. We’ll also think about the place of Hebrew and Jewish culture in our lives.

What kind of ceremony feels right for me?

A Bar or Bat Mitzvah ceremony is a way of marking an important moment in our lives.

But what is a ceremony? Why do we have them? And how can we create one that feels meaningful and true to who we are?

Participants will have a chance to think about these questions and take part in shaping the ceremony itself.

Making a difference

Growing up also means discovering that we can make a difference.

Together, we’ll think about responsibility, tikkun olam and the kind of world we want to live in - and turn some of those ideas into a shared community project.

A group journey

This isn't a course where you simply come, learn and go home.

The group is an important part of the experience. Participants have the opportunity to make new friends, share their stories, ask questions, work together and learn from one another.

The program also includes opportunities to connect with the local Jewish community and meet other young people.

And throughout the process, there are moments for parents and families to join in too.

Parents are part of the journey too

Parents will be invited to take part in some of the sessions and activities and, especially, to help with the family research.

This is a wonderful opportunity to share stories, memories and conversations that might not otherwise happen - and to experience this important stage of growing up together.

And at the end?

The journey comes together in a shared Bar & Bat Mitzvah celebration.

Participants will have the opportunity to share what they have discovered, created and experienced throughout the program and to celebrate this important milestone together as a community.

A journey of discovering where we come from, who we are and who we want to become.
