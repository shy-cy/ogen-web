About the class

A Journey of Discovery, Belonging and Growing Up

The transition from childhood to adolescence is a significant milestone in every young person's life. A Bar or Bat Mitzvah is an opportunity to mark this moment, but it is about so much more than the ceremony. It is a chance to explore who we are, where we come from, and where we belong.

The OGEN Centre’s Bnei Mitzvah Programme invites young people to embark on a journey of self-discovery, exploring identity, family, friendship, responsibility, independence and belonging, both as individuals and as part of a community.

Through conversations, creative projects, hands-on activities, family research and shared experiences, participants will explore their connections to their families, their community, Israel and Jewish culture. Along the way, they will reflect on some of the big questions that come with growing up: What makes us who we are? Where do we feel at home? What matters most to us? And how do we want to contribute to the world around us?

What We'll Explore Together

Me and My Bnei Mitzvah Year

What does becoming a Bar or Bat Mitzvah mean to me? What does mitzvah mean, and how can its meaning find a place in my everyday life?

We’ll get to know one another, explore our names and the stories behind them, and discover the unique strengths and talents each of us brings to the group.

Where I Come From: My Family Story

Every family has a story. Each of us is part of it.

Through interviews, photographs, writing and storytelling, we’ll explore our family histories and discover how they have shaped who we are. Where possible, we’ll also bring family members in Israel into the journey, giving participants the chance to connect with their roots across generations and borders.

From Our Family Stories to the Story of a People

We’ll share the stories we’ve uncovered and look for the connections between them.

Together, we’ll discover how our personal and family histories are part of something bigger: the stories of our community, the Jewish people and the places we call home.

Growing Up: Who Am I Becoming?

What does it mean to grow up? Is adulthood simply a matter of age? And what do independence and responsibility look like in practice?

We’ll explore what it means to take responsibility for ourselves and for the people around us, including our families, friends and community. We’ll also think about how to put our values into practice and make choices that reflect the people we want to become.

Me and My Friends

Adolescence is a time when friendships evolve, relationships change and we begin to discover the people we want to be.

We’ll talk about friendship, the people who influence us, and those who inspire us. As part of the programme, we’ll also meet inspiring members of our own community and hear their stories.

Finding My Place: The Many Sides of Who I Am

Who am I, and where do I belong? How do the different parts of my life come together to shape my identity?

We’ll explore the circles we belong to, including our families, friends, community, Israel and the wider Jewish world. We’ll consider how each one contributes to our sense of who we are.

Creating a Ceremony That Reflects Me

A Bar or Bat Mitzvah ceremony is a chance to pause, reflect and mark an important milestone.

We’ll explore what makes a ceremony meaningful, why traditions matter, and how to create a Jewish celebration that feels personal and authentic. Participants will have a say in shaping the ceremony and deciding how they want to mark this important moment in their lives.

Making a Difference

Growing up also means recognising that we can make a difference in the world around us.

We’ll explore values, responsibility and Tikkun Olam, the Jewish idea of helping to repair the world. Working together, the group will develop a project or initiative that turns ideas into action.

More Than a Programme: A Journey We Take Together

At its heart, this is a shared journey of discovery. Over time, the group will become a place where participants feel comfortable asking questions, sharing ideas, listening to one another, laughing, exploring and learning about themselves and each other.

Along the way, they’ll make new friends, connect with other young people in the local community, and build relationships that become an important part of the experience.

The journey culminates in a shared community Bar and Bat Mitzvah celebration, bringing everyone together to mark this milestone.

The Parents’ Role

Parents are an important part of the journey, too.

They’ll have opportunities to join selected sessions, help with the family research project, and support their children as they take part in activities and explore new ideas.

The aim is not simply to prepare each child for a ceremony. It’s to create a meaningful family experience built around shared memories, connection, belonging and growing up.

Celebrating the Journey

The programme concludes with a shared Bar and Bat Mitzvah celebration and ceremony, bringing together everything the participants have discovered, explored and created along the way.

It’s a moment to celebrate not only the milestone itself, but also the journey that led to it: who they are, where they come from, the connections they’ve made, and the exciting road ahead.
