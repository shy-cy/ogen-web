About the class

The Bible, Big Questions and a Different Perspective<p>We are proud to invite <strong>Yifat Hald</strong> to lead OGEN Centre’s Community Beit Midrash.</p><p>Yifat is a well-known member of the community and is highly regarded for her approach to teaching the Bible, bringing curiosity, depth and the ability to look at familiar stories from new and intriguing perspectives.</p><p>The Community Beit Midrash will take place in two groups: <strong>a youth and young adults group for ages 15 to 25, and an adult group.</strong> The content and discussions in each group will be adapted to the age and characteristics of its participants.</p><p>Together, we will open the Bible, return to stories we think we know and discover that they are far more complex and fascinating than they may first appear. We will explore different versions of familiar stories, compare sources, ask questions about identity, faith, society and gender, and consider what these ancient stories can tell us about the world we live in today.</p><p>This is a <strong>community-based, open and thought-provoking Beit Midrash</strong>, a space where we can learn, ask questions, share ideas and even disagree.</p><p>No previous knowledge of the Bible is required. All you need is curiosity and a willingness to look at familiar stories with fresh eyes.</p><p><strong>Six sessions. Ancient stories. Questions that are still relevant today.</strong></p><h3>During the six sessions, we will explore:</h3><p><strong>Two Stories, One Creator</strong></p><p> A closer look at the two creation stories and what we can learn from the differences between them.</p><p><strong>The Storm That Came Together</strong></p><p> Two flood stories that changed the world, and the fascinating connection between Genesis and the Epic of Gilgamesh.</p><p><strong>Jewish DNA</strong></p><p> The genes we inherited from the fathers and mothers of our people, and what biblical stories tell us about our identity.</p><p><strong>The Soul of a King</strong></p><p> The light and shadows in the life of King David, one of the most complex and fascinating figures in the Bible.</p><p><strong>The Paradox of Ecclesiastes</strong></p><p> Between deep despair and practical optimism, and what Ecclesiastes has to say about life.</p><p><strong>Created in God's Image or From His Rib?</strong></p><p> What really lies behind the story of the creation of woman, and what does it have to do with the question of who is "the man" in the home?</p><p>The six session topics are based on the programme provided.</p>
