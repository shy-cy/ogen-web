About the class

Ayekha?<h3>Ancient Wisdom, Modern Life</h3><p>How can stories written thousands of years ago speak to us today? What can they teach us about ourselves, our relationships and the world we live in?</p><p>Ayekha? is a monthly gathering in Russian where we explore the weekly Torah portion and discover how its stories, ideas and timeless questions connect with our lives today.</p><p>Together, we will delve into the text, share perspectives and explore the values and questions at its heart. We will look at familiar stories in new ways, reflect on their meaning in the context of modern life, and consider how Jewish tradition can offer fresh insights into the challenges and experiences we face today.</p><p>More than a study session, Ayekha? is a space for open conversation, curiosity and shared reflection. A place to engage with Jewish tradition, hear different interpretations and develop our own understanding through learning together.</p><p>The gatherings take place once a month and are conducted in Russian. They are open to anyone who is curious about the weekly Torah portion and interested in discovering new connections between Jewish tradition and contemporary life. No previous knowledge is needed, just an open mind and a willingness to explore.</p><p>Ancient stories. Today's questions. New perspectives.</p>
