Children's Hebrew Class

About the class

A weekly class based on the official curriculum of the World Zionist Organization. Children learn Hebrew through play, song and creative work, in small age-appropriate groups.

What's included

  • A weekly 60-minute session
  • All learning and activity materials
  • Groups by age, up to 10 children per group
  • A parents’ meeting mid-season

What to bring

A notebook and something to write with. We provide everything else.

Frequently asked questions

What happens if we miss a session? You can make it up with a parallel group, arranged in advance.

What is the cancellation policy? Cancelling up to two weeks before the season starts entitles you to a full refund.
