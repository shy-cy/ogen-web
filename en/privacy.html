Privacy Policy

The text below is the approved version. It is not yet in effect: Ogen's registration services are not yet open.

Last updated: 1st September 2026

1. Who we are

Ogen Center ("Ogen," "we," "us") is a community centre based in Limassol, Cyprus, offering educational and cultural activities.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal data when you use ogen.cy, create an account, register a participant, or otherwise interact with our registration services.

Ogen is currently operated through a legal entity within the Ogen/Rolito group. The final legal entity responsible for the service may change. We will update this Policy with the relevant legal entity details once finalised.

Contact for privacy matters: contact-us@ogen.cy

2. What data we collect

Account holders (guardians): name, email address, phone number, securely stored password, preferred language, registration/participation information, and payment-related records such as amounts charged, amounts paid, and transaction references. We do not store full payment card numbers; payments are processed through Stripe.

Participants (children): first and last name, date of birth, registration and attendance history, and photo/media consent status. We do not create separate accounts, login credentials, or independent digital identities for children.

3. Why we collect personal data and our legal bases

We use personal data to create and manage accounts (contract); register participants (contract); check age eligibility (legitimate interests including safety and programme suitability); send confirmations, reminders and important notices (contract); process and record payments (contract and legal obligations); maintain financial/accounting records (legal obligation); manage guardian and participant records (contract and legitimate interests); use photographs/video in Ogen's own promotional or community materials (consent); and operate, secure and improve the website (legitimate interests and, where applicable, consent for non-essential cookies and analytics).

Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time. Withdrawal does not affect processing carried out lawfully before withdrawal.

4. Children's data and guardian responsibility

Our registration services are designed for adults who register children or other participants under their care. When registering a child, the guardian confirms that they are the child's parent, legal guardian, or otherwise authorised to provide the child's information and make relevant decisions on the child's behalf. The child's information is maintained as part of the guardian's account and used only for the purposes described in this Policy. We do not provide children with independent user accounts or login credentials. A second guardian can be linked only after an invitation from the primary guardian and active acceptance by the second guardian.

5. Who we share personal data with

We do not sell personal data. We share data only where necessary to operate services, process registrations/payments, communicate with users, comply with legal obligations, or protect our rights and users.

Resend: email delivery. Netlify: website hosting and infrastructure; it does not process or store users' full payment card information. Stripe: payment processing; Ogen does not store full card numbers. Google Analytics: website analytics, subject to cookie consent settings. Cyprus authorities and other public bodies: where required by law.

We require service providers to comply with applicable data protection requirements and use appropriate data-processing arrangements where required.

6. International data transfers

Some providers may process personal data on servers or in locations outside Cyprus or the European Economic Area (EEA). Where data is transferred outside the EEA, we take steps to ensure compliance with applicable data protection law and appropriate safeguards, which may include an adequacy decision, Standard Contractual Clauses, or another legally recognised transfer mechanism.

7. Cookies and Google Analytics

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies. Technically necessary cookies may be used without consent where permitted by law. We also use Google Analytics to understand website usage and improve the website. Where consent is required, non-essential analytics cookies are activated only after consent through our cookie mechanism. Visitors can refuse or withdraw consent at any time. We do not use Google Analytics to intentionally collect sensitive personal information about participants.

8. How long we keep personal data

We keep personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law. Account information is retained while an account is active; participant information while needed for registration, participation and related records; unnecessary data is deleted or anonymised after closure/removal. Financial, accounting, tax and transaction records may be retained longer where required by law or necessary for legal claims. Email logs may be retained by our email provider for a limited period. Cookie and analytics information is retained according to the relevant service's settings and retention periods. We periodically review and delete or anonymise information no longer required.

9. Photos and media

We may photograph or record video during Ogen activities for community, educational, archival or promotional purposes. Where intended use of a child's image/video requires consent, guardians may be asked to provide it separately. Refusing photo/media consent does not prevent participation unless otherwise clearly stated for a particular programme. Where consent is not provided, we take reasonable steps to avoid intentionally including that child in published materials. Consent may be withdrawn at contact-us@ogen.cy. Withdrawal applies to future use; it may not be possible to recall or remove material already printed, distributed, published or otherwise made available.

10. Your rights

Under applicable data protection law, including GDPR where applicable, you may have rights to access, correct, delete (subject to legal retention), restrict or object to certain processing, receive certain data in a portable format, and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. Contact contact-us@ogen.cy. We may verify identity before responding. You may also complain to the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection in Cyprus.

11. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure. Measures may include access controls, secure authentication, encrypted connections, appropriate permissions and secure password storage. No online service is completely risk-free. If a personal data breach occurs, we will assess it and take steps required by law, including notifying the supervisory authority and/or affected individuals where legally required.

12. Data Protection Officer and privacy contact

Ogen does not currently have a separately appointed Data Protection Officer. For privacy questions or requests concerning personal data, contact contact-us@ogen.cy. If a DPO is appointed in the future, we will update this Policy.

13. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Policy to reflect changes to services, technology, legal requirements or processing practices. For material changes, we will update the Last updated date and, where appropriate, provide additional notice.

14. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal data: contact-us@ogen.cy
