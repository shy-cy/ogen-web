1. Who we are

Ogen Center ("Ogen," "we," "us") is a community centre based in Limassol, Cyprus, offering educational and cultural activities. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect personal data when you use our website (ogen.cy), create an account, or register for an activity.

2. What data we collect

Account holder (guardian)

  • Name, email address, phone number
  • Password (stored securely, never in plain text)
  • Preferred language
  • Payment-related records (amounts owed and paid; we do not store full card numbers)

Participants (children) linked to an account

  • First and last name
  • Date of birth
  • Dietary requirements and known allergies
  • Photo/media consent status
  • Emergency contact information, where provided
  • Registration and attendance history

We do not create separate accounts, login credentials, or independent digital identities for minors. A child's data exists only as a profile linked to one or more guardian accounts.

4. Guardian consent for minors' data

Because our participants are minors, all data about a child is provided and consented to by a parent or legal guardian. By registering a child, you confirm that you are their parent or legal guardian, or otherwise authorised to provide their information and consent on their behalf.

A second guardian may be linked to a child's record only after the first guardian sends an invitation and the second guardian actively accepts it. We do not permit any guardian to link themselves to a child without this deliberate, mutual step.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. We share data only with:

  • Resend (email delivery provider) - to send account, registration, and reminder emails
  • Netlify (hosting and storage provider) - where our systems run and data is stored
  • [PAYMENT PROVIDER, if/when introduced]
  • Cyprus authorities, where required by law

All processors are bound by data processing agreements consistent with GDPR requirements.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account and participant data is retained while the account is active.
  • If an account is closed or a participant is removed, we retain minimal records as required for financial/legal record-keeping ([RETENTION PERIOD] years), after which data is deleted.
  • Email delivery logs are retained by our email provider for a limited period (currently up to 30 days) and, where relevant to registration history, in our own systems as described above.

7. Photos and media

We may take photographs or video at activities. Guardians are asked to indicate photo consent per child. Where consent is not given, we take reasonable steps to exclude that child from published materials. Consent can be withdrawn at any time by contacting us.

8. Your rights

Under applicable data protection law (including GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you or your child
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of data, subject to our legal retention obligations
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing carried out before withdrawal

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL]. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Cyprus Commissioner for Personal Data Protection.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including access controls on our administrative systems and secure storage of passwords. No system is entirely without risk, and we will notify affected individuals and authorities as required by law in the event of a data breach.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be indicated with a new "last updated" date. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
