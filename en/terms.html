2. Who may create an account

You must be at least 18 years old and a parent or legal guardian of any participant you register. Accounts are held by adults only; participants (children) do not hold their own login credentials.

3. Linking a second guardian

A child's record may be linked to a second guardian account, in addition to the first (primary) guardian who created it. This requires the primary guardian to send an invitation to the second guardian, who must accept it before the link is created. Ogen may, at its discretion, manually adjust guardian links in cases of lost access or disputes.

Submitting a registration reserves a place for the participant but does not guarantee enrolment. Some activities require our approval before a registration is confirmed; others are automatically approved. We may decline a registration, including on the basis of age eligibility, capacity, or other programme requirements, at our discretion.

A pending registration that is not approved or rejected within [14] days (or the period stated for a specific activity) will automatically expire, releasing the reserved place.

5. Fees and payment

Activity fees consist of a registration fee and, where applicable, a course fee. The specific amounts, and any registration-fee cutoff date after which the registration fee becomes non-refundable, are shown on each activity's page at the time of registration.

Whichever guardian account submits and pays for a registration is responsible for that payment; fees are not pooled or shared automatically across guardian accounts linked to the same participant.

6. Cancellations and credit

We do not offer cash refunds. Where a cancellation is eligible for compensation under this section, the amount is credited to your Ogen account as wallet credit, which does not expire and can be used toward future activities. Wallet credit cannot be exchanged for cash or transferred to another account.

  • Cancelling before an activity begins: full credit for amounts paid, subject to the registration-fee cutoff date shown on the activity page.
  • Cancelling after an activity has begun: credit is calculated according to the cancellation policy stated on that activity's page (either a flat rate or a rate proportional to remaining sessions), up until the activity's cancellation cutoff date.
  • After the cancellation cutoff date: no further credit is available for that registration. We may still remove a participant from an activity after this date where necessary (for example, for safety reasons), without credit.

Specific dates and the applicable policy for each activity are shown at the time of registration and are fixed to the terms in force at that time.

7. Drop-in / pay-per-session activities

Some activities are billed per session rather than as a single course fee. The cancellation terms in Section 6 do not apply to these; instead, the specific terms for missing or cancelling a booked session are shown on that activity's page.
